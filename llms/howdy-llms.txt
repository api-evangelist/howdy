# Howdy

> Howdy is a nearshore talent platform and employer of record (EOR) that connects US
> companies with senior software engineers, data, DevOps, design, and product professionals
> across Latin America. Howdy handles recruiting, vetting, payroll, benefits, equipment,
> compliance, and team performance coaching. Founded in 2018 in Austin, Texas; Y Combinator
> W21. Howdy is a services/staffing business and does not publish a public developer API.
